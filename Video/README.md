# Video

`126003133_Video_v1.1.mp4` is the demonstration video (MP4, 1920×1080, 9 min 45 s, 44 MB).

It walks through the input data, the architecture and RAG pipeline, indexing a document, the
extracted architecture and consistency checks, grounded answers with citations and source passages,
refusals of unsupported questions, revision comparison, human review with the audit log, export, and
the evaluation results.

To fit the 5–10 minute limit, waiting periods in the recording are fast-forwarded and narration is
played slightly faster; nothing shown on screen was removed.
